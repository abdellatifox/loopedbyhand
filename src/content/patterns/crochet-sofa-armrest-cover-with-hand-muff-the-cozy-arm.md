This crochet Sofa Armrest Cover with Hand Muff (The Cozy Arm) pattern makes a practical and cozy addition to your couch. Designed to provide warmth to your arms while lounging, it features a built-in muff for comfort. This project involves simple stitches like double crochet and single crochet, making it enjoyable and straightforward to create.

This cozy armrest cover is perfect for anyone looking to enhance their home decor while improving their crochet skills. You’ll gain experience working with basic stitches, increasing your confidence in crochet.

- Functional design – it neatly combines an armrest cover with a hand muff for added warmth.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is easy to follow and primarily uses double crochet and single crochet stitches. It’s a great way to practice and develop your skills.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sc – single crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Sofa Armrest Cover: 24 in (60 cm) long × 8 in (20 cm) wide
- Finished Muff: 10 in (25 cm) wide × 12 in (30 cm) deep
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 4 in (10 cm) in double crochet stitch.

Make a small swatch to ensure your gauge matches for the best fit. If necessary, adjust your hook size for the proper gauge.

## How to Crochet a Sofa Armrest Cover with Hand Muff (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Sofa Armrest Cover
The body is worked in rows, starting from a foundation chain.
1. **Foundation Chain**: Ch 50.
2. **Row 1**: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (48 dc)
3. **Row 2**: Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in each dc across. Turn. (48 dc)
4. **Rows 3–12**: Repeat Row 2 (total of 10 rows).
5. **Row 13**: Ch 1, sc in each dc across. Turn. (48 sc)
6. **Row 14**: Ch 1, sc in first 10 sc, ch 5 (for buttonhole), skip next sc, sc in remaining sc. Turn. (47 sc total)
7. **Row 15**: Ch 3, dc in each sc across. Turn. (47 dc)
8. **Rows 16–22**: Repeat Row 5 (total of 7 rows).
9. **Fasten Off**: Leave a long tail for sewing.

### Step 2: Muff
The muff is created as a separate rectangle.
1. **Foundation Chain**: Ch 35.
2. **Row 1**: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. Turn. (33 dc)
3. **Rows 2–23**: Repeat Row 2 for a total of 23 rows.
4. **Fasten Off**: Leave a long tail for sewing.

### Step 3: Assembly
Now we’ll bring everything together.
1. **Joining the Muff**: Fold the muff rectangle in half, short edges together. Sew sides using slip stitch or whip stitch to form a tube.
2. **Attach Muff to Armrest Cover**: Position the muff in the center of the armrest cover, approximately 10 in (25 cm) from one end. Use the long tail from the armrest cover to sew the muff securely.
3. **Button Closure**: Position the large button on the armrest cover where the buttonhole is located. Sew the button in place.
4. Weave in all remaining ends with the tapestry needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Sofa Armrest Cover
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the right fit.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows and avoid losing your place.
- Don’t hesitate to undo and redo if something doesn’t look right; practice makes perfect!

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This can happen if you’re holding the yarn too tightly. Try to relax your grip.
- The shape looks off. – Make sure you're counting your stitches correctly and tension is consistent throughout.
- I have gaps at my stitches. – Ensure you're pulling the yarn tightly when joining or transitioning to another row to avoid gaps.

## How to Care for Your Sofa Armrest Cover
For the best results, hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid direct sunlight to prevent fading.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Absolutely! You can choose any worsted weight yarn you like for this project, keeping in mind the color combinations that suit your decor.

### How do I resize the armrest cover?
To resize, simply adjust the foundation chain to your desired length and maintain the stitch count for rows according to your gauge.

### How long will it take to make this?
The estimated time is about 3 hours – 2 hours for the armrest cover and 1 hour for the muff.
