## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (sc 2 together)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Dimensions:**
  - Newborn: 13–14 in (33–36 cm) circumference
  - Infant: 14–15 in (36–38 cm) circumference
  - Baby: 15–16 in (38–41 cm) circumference
  - Toddler: 16–17 in (41–43 cm) circumference
  - Preschool: 17–18 in (43–46 cm) circumference
- **Gauge:** 14 sc and 10 rows = 4 in (10 cm)

*To ensure a proper fit, it's essential to check your gauge. Adjust your hook size as necessary if your gauge does not match the recommended stitches and rows.*

## How to Crochet a Baby Pom-Pom Hat (Step by Step)
The body of the hat is worked in rounds, starting at the center.

### Step 1: Newborn Size (0-3 months)
1. **Foundation Round:** Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.
2. Round 1: Ch 1, 8 sc in ring. (8 sts)
3. Round 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. (16 sts)
4. Round 3: Ch 1, *sc in next sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. (24 sts)
5. Round 4: Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sc, inc in next sc; repeat from * around. (32 sts)
6. Rounds 5–8: Ch 1, sc in each sc around. (32 sts)
7. **Finishing:** Fasten off, weave in ends.

*You now have the hat body completed for the newborn size!*

### Step 2: Other Sizes
- **Infant (3-6 months):** Repeat steps with Rounds 5–9. (36 total stitches)
- **Baby (6-12 months):** Rounds 5–10. (40 total stitches)
- **Toddler (1-2 years):** Rounds 5–11. (44 total stitches)
- **Preschool (3-4 years):** Rounds 5–12. (48 total stitches)

### Step 3: Pom-Pom Method
1. Using a pom-pom maker or cardboard templates, wrap the yarn securely in the middle and trim the ends to create a fluffy pom-pom.
2. Attach the pom-pom to the top of the hat, sewing securely with a yarn needle.

### Step 4: Assembly & Finishing
- Attach any embellishments (like buttons) using the yarn needle.
- Weave in all loose ends using the yarn needle.
- Blocking is optional; lightly block if necessary to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Baby Pom-Pom Hat
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure a good fit.
- Don’t pull your stitches too tightly, as this can affect the size.
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of the beginning of each round.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This can cause the hat to fit poorly. Try to relax your tension while you work.
- The shape looks off. – This might happen if you skip rounds or do not follow the increasing pattern. Double-check each round.
- The hat is too small or too large. – Make sure to check your gauge and adjust your hook size accordingly.

## How to Care for Your Baby Pom-Pom Hat
Hand wash only and lay flat to dry. Avoid machine drying to prevent shrinking.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! You can choose any worsted weight yarn you like, but keep in mind this might change the final size and feel of your hat.

### How do I make it larger or smaller?
To resize, simply adjust the number of rounds or stitches in each round according to the desired size while maintaining the increase pattern.

### How do I attach the pom-pom securely?
Use a yarn needle to sew through the center of the pom-pom, then secure it firmly to the top of the hat. Make sure to tie off the yarn well.
