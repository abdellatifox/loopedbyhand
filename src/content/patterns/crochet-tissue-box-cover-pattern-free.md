## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- bobble – (yarn over, insert hook into stitch, yarn over, pull up a loop) 4 times, yarn over, pull through all 5 loops on hook
- sl st – slip stitch

## How to Make a Bobble Stitch
1. Yarn over and insert your hook into the designated stitch.
2. Yarn over again and pull up a loop. You will have three loops on your hook.
3. Repeat this process three more times in the same stitch. You should have five loops on your hook.
4. Yarn over and pull through all five loops, completing the bobble stitch.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### How to Crochet a Tissue Box Cover (Step by Step)

**Body of the Tissue Box Cover**
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 31. (30 sts)
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (30 sts)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sts)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 2, turn. Bobble in 1st st, *sc in next 3 sts; bobble in next st.* Repeat from * to * until 1 st remaining, sc in last st. (7 bobbles, 22 sc)
5. **Row 4:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sts)
6. **Row 5:** Ch 2, turn. Bobble in 1st st, *sc in next 3 sts; bobble in next st.* Repeat from * to * until 1 st remaining, sc in last st. (7 bobbles, 22 sc)
7. **Rows 6–12:** Repeat Rows 4 and 5 until you have a total of 12 rows with the last row ending with a sc row. (Final Row 12 = 30 sts)
8. **Row 13:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sts)
9. **Row 14:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sts)
10. **Row 15:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st but leave the last 3 sts, turn. (27 sts)

**Forming the Sides**
11. **Row 16:** Ch 1, sc in each of the remaining 27 sts from row 15, then sc in the first 3 sts of Row 2 (corner). (30 sts total)
12. **Rows 17–30:** Continue to sc around the entire square. This creates the sides of the tissue box cover. Slip stitch to join to the beginning sc of Row 17.

### Assembly & Finishing
- Weave in any yarn tails using a yarn needle.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Tissue Box Cover
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of your rows, especially when starting the sides.
- Don’t rush; take your time to ensure each bobble is evenly spaced.
- Regularly measure your piece to confirm it fits the standard tissue box dimensions.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This could be due to using a hook that’s too small. Try using a larger hook for a looser gauge.
- The shape looks off. – Make sure to count your stitches after each row to ensure you haven't missed any.
- The bobble stitches aren't popping. – This might mean you need to loosen your tension slightly while making the bobbles.

## How to Care for Your Tissue Box Cover
Machine wash on a gentle cycle and tumble dry on low or lay flat to dry. This will keep your cover looking fresh and clean.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?

### How do I resize the pattern?
To resize, adjust the number of chains in the foundation row. Just remember to keep your gauge in mind!

### Will the bobble stitch make it difficult to fit over a tissue box?
No, the bobble stitch adds a fun texture, but it won’t affect the fit. Just ensure your stitch count is accurate.
