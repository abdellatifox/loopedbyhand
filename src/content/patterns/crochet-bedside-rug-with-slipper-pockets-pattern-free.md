Not only does it provide a cozy place to step out of bed, but the integrated pockets keep your slippers conveniently close by. You'll be using basic double crochet stitches, along with foundation single crochet and reverse single crochet for the edging. 

This project is perfect for anyone looking to enhance their crochet skills. As you work through the steps, you'll gain confidence in creating larger projects and learn to incorporate handy features like pockets.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sk – skip
- sl st – slip stitch
- fsc – foundation single crochet
- rsc – reverse single crochet

**Foundation Single Crochet (Fsc):**
1. Make a slip knot and chain 16.
2. Insert the hook into the first chain.
3. Yarn over and pull up a loop (2 loops on hook).
4. Yarn over again and pull through both loops on the hook. This forms 1 fsc.
5. Repeat for each chain across.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions:
  - Rug: 76 cm × 61 cm (30 in × 24 in)
  - Pocket: 41 cm × 20 cm (16 in × 8 in)
- Gauge: 14 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in).
- To check gauge, crochet a swatch. Adjust your hook size if necessary.

## How to Crochet a Bedside Rug (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Create the Rug Base
The body is worked in rows, starting with a long chain.
1. Using Color A, ch 101.
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (99 sts)
3. Rows 2–40: Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (99 sts)

### Step 2: Create Slipper Pockets
We’ll make two pockets for your slippers, worked separately.
1. For the left pocket:
   - Using Color B, ch 41.
   - Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook, dc in each ch across. (39 sts)
   - Rows 2–39: Ch 3, turn. Dc in each st across. (39 sts)
   - Finish off, leaving a long tail for sewing.
2. For the right pocket: Repeat the same steps as above.

### Step 3: Assembly of Rug and Pockets
Next, we’ll attach the pockets to the rug:
- Position the left pocket 15 cm (6 in) from the left edge and the right pocket 15 cm (6 in) from the right edge.
- Use the long tail to sew each pocket to the rug base.
- Turn edges of pockets under and sew if using fabric lining (optional).

### Step 4: Edging
The edging gives a nice finish to the rug.
1. Using Color C, join yarn with a sl st at any corner of the rug.
2. Work 1 round of rsc around the entire rug, spacing evenly. Join with a sl st to the first rsc.

## Assembly & Finishing
Once you're finished crocheting, weave in all ends using a tapestry needle. If necessary, block the rug to shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Bedside Rug
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the rug fits the desired size.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rows or to mark the beginning of the pocket areas.
- Keep an eye on your tension; consistency will make your finished rug look neat.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My starting chain is too tight. This can make it hard to work double crochet rows. Try loosening your tension a bit.
- The rug looks uneven or warped. This can happen if your tension varies too much. Focus on keeping even tension throughout the project.
- The pockets are not the right size. Make sure to measure your pockets and adjust your chains accordingly.

## How to Care for Your Bedside Rug
For best results, machine wash your rug in cold water and tumble dry on low or lay it flat to dry. Avoid bleach to keep the colors looking fresh.
