## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions:
  - Width: 30 cm (12 in)
  - Height: 15 cm (6 in)
  - Depth: 20 cm (8 in)
- Gauge: 10 dc and 5 rows = 10 cm (4 in). Be sure to check your gauge to ensure your basket will be the correct size.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Part 1: Main Basket Body
The body is worked in rounds, starting with a foundation round.
1. **Foundation Round**: Ch 42, join with sl st to form a ring. (42 sts)
2. **Round 1**: Ch 3 (counts as dc), dc in each ch around, join with sl st to top of ch 3. (42 sts)
3. **Rounds 2–10**: Repeat Round 1 for 9 more rounds. (42 sts each) 
   - At the end of Round 10, your basket should be approximately 15 cm high.

### Part 2: Bottom Panel
The bottom panel creates a sturdy base.
1. **Base Circle**: Ch 1, *2 dc in next st, dc in next st, repeat from * around. (63 sts)
2. **Next Round**: Ch 1, *2 dc in next st, dc in next 2 sts, repeat from * around. (84 sts)
3. **Next Round**: Ch 1, *2 dc in next st, dc in next 3 sts, repeat from * around. (105 sts)
4. **Final Round**: Ch 1, sl st around to join but leave off the last stitch for the handle. (105 sts + 1 omitted)
   - **Tip:** Insert stiff interfacing or cardboard cut to size for support.

### Part 3: Handle
The handle is added for easy accessibility.
1. **Handle Chain**: Ch 30, sl st to the last dc on the opposite side of the gap created for the handle.
2. **Join Handle**: Ch 1, sc down back along the chain and finish with sl st into the basket for sturdiness.
3. **Finish the Handle**: Repeat the process on the opposite side for symmetry.

### Assembly & Finishing
Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle to neaten your basket. Make sure the base support is securely held in place with pins. It’s a good idea to block the basket to maintain its shape when filled.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Slide Bin
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds if needed.
- Count your stitches as you go to ensure your basket remains the correct size.
- Adjust your tension if your stitches feel too tight or too loose.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This could be due to tension. Try crocheting loosely for a more comfortable gauge.
- "The shape looks off." – Check if you're skipping stitches or if your base isn’t flat. Lay it out on a flat surface to inspect.
- "I can't see my stitches." – Make sure you're using good lighting and consider using a brighter color yarn for visibility while you work.

## How to Care for Your Slide Bin
Hand wash your basket in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid using bleach as that can affect the yarn's color and texture.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes, you can use any bulky yarn. Just make sure it retains the correct gauge for the basket size.

### How do I resize the basket?
To adjust the size, increase or decrease the number of foundation chains in multiples of three, keeping in mind to adjust the bottom panel as well.
