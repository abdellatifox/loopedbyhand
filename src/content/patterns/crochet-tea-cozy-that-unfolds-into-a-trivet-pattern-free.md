## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet  
- sp – space  
- inc – increase  
- dec – decrease  

**Shell Stitch:** (5 dc in the same st) to create a cluster that will be used in the body of the cozy.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:**  
  - Diameter of tea cozy: 12 inches (30.5 cm)  
  - Diameter of lid: 8 inches (20 cm)  
- **Gauge:** 14 dc and 8 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in dc using 5.0 mm hook (H). To ensure your cozy fits correctly, make a small swatch. Adjust your hook size if your gauge does not match.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### How to Crochet a Tea Cozy (Step by Step)

#### Body of Tea Cozy
The body is worked in rounds, starting at the center.

1. **Starting with Color A (5.0 mm hook)**  
   - Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring.  
   - (1) Ch 3 (counts as first dc), 11 dc in ring. Join with sl st to top of ch 3. (12 sts)

2. **Round 2:**  
   - Ch 3, (1 dc in next st, 2 dc in next st) Repeat around. Join with sl st. (18 sts)

3. **Round 3:**  
   - Ch 3, (1 dc in next 2 sts, 2 dc in next st) Repeat around. Join. (24 sts)

4. **Round 4:**  
   - Change to Color B, ch 3, *shell stitch in next st, skip 1 st*. Repeat around and finish with a sl st in top of ch 3. (12 shells)

5. **Round 5:**  
   - Change to Color A, ch 3, (1 dc in shell st, 1 dc in next st) Repeat around. Join. (36 sts)

6. **Round 6:**  
   - Change to Color C, ch 3, *shell stitch in next st, skip 1 st*. Repeat around and finish with sl st in top of ch 3. (12 shells)

7. **Round 7:**  
   - Change to Color A, ch 2, (1 sc in next 2 dcs, ch 2) Repeat around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)

8. **Round 8:**  
   - Ch 1, (1 sc, 1 ch in next ch two sp) Repeat around. Join with sl st. (36 sts)  
   - Finish off and weave in ends.

#### Lid of Tea Cozy
The lid is also worked in rounds, starting with the same method as the body.

1. **Using Color A (5.0 mm hook):**  
   - Repeat rounds 1 to 3 as above, creating a circle that is 8 inches (20 cm) in diameter.

#### Assembly
- Align the cozy body with the lid. 
- Using Color A, sl st around the edge to attach the lid to the body, ensuring it is securely fastened and visible stitching is minimized. 
- Join, leaving a small opening for the button (optional).

## Beginner Tips for a Great Tea Cozy
- Use a stitch marker to mark the beginning of rounds – this will help keep your rounds even.
- Crochet loosely in shell stitches – tight stitches can lead to uneven rounds.
- Check your work regularly – count your stitches as you go to avoid mistakes.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This could create uneven rounds or make it difficult to work. Try using a larger hook to loosen your tension.
- The shape looks off. – This is often due to missed stitches or uneven tension. Count your stitches after each round to ensure accuracy.
- The colors bleed when washed. – Always hand wash your cozy with mild detergent to avoid color bleeding. 

## How to Care for Your Tea Cozy
Hand wash with mild detergent and lay flat to dry. This will help maintain the cozy's shape and color.
