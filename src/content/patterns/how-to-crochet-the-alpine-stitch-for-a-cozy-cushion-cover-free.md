## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- sc – single crochet  
- dc – double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- alpine stitch – (1) work 1 dc in the next st; (2) skip 1 st; (3) work 1 sc in the next st (this creates the alpine stitch). Repeat from * to * across the row.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished size:** 18 inches x 18 inches (45 cm x 45 cm)
- **Gauge:** 12 sts and 12 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in alpine stitch.

## How to Crochet a Cozy Cushion Cover (Step by Step)
#### Cushion Cover Body
The body is worked in rows, starting with a foundation chain. 
1. **Foundation Row:** Ch 40. (40 ch)
2. **Row 1:** Dc in 4th ch from the hook (counts as first dc), dc in next 2 ch, *skip 1 ch, sc in next ch, skip 1 ch, dc in next 3 ch; repeat from * across to last 3 ch, dc in last 3 ch. (40 sts)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 3 (counts as first dc), dc in next 2 sts, *skip 1 st, sc in next st, skip 1 st, dc in next 3 sts; repeat from * across to last 3 sts, dc in last 3 sts. (40 sts)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 3, turn. Repeat Row 2. (40 sts)
5. **Repeat Row 2** for a total of **26 rows** or until work measures 18 inches (45 cm) in height, ending with Row 2.

**Note:** You will have a total of 26 rows of the alpine stitch. 

### Assembly & Finishing
To finish your cushion cover, we’ll fold and seam the pieces.
- Fold the rectangle in half with the right sides together, aligning the edges.
- Join the sides using a slip stitch or single crochet, leaving one side open for inserting the cushion.
- Turn the cover right side out.
- Insert the cushion into the cover.
- Optionally, slip stitch the open side closed or use buttons or a zipper for easy removal.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Cushion Cover
- Count your chains accurately at the beginning; this sets you up for success.
- Keep your tension even for a neat finish; practice makes perfect!
- Block your finished cover to enhance the shape and texture.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My edges look uneven." – This might be due to inconsistent stitch counts. Double-check your stitches in each row.
- "The cushion cover is too small." – Make sure to check your gauge. Adjust your hook size if necessary.
- "The pattern looks bumpy." – An uneven tension can cause this. Try to maintain a consistent tension as you work.

## How to Care for Your Cushion Cover
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid tumble drying to maintain the integrity of your stitches.
