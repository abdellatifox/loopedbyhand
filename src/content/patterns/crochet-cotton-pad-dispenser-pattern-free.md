## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- rnd – round
- sp – space

## How to Check Your Gauge
- To check gauge, crochet a 4-inch (10 cm) square in double crochet and measure. If your gauge does not match, switch to a larger or smaller hook accordingly.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Size:** Approximately 15 cm (6 in) tall and 10 cm (4 in) in diameter
- **Gauge:** 12 dc and 7 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### How to Crochet a Pad Tower (Step by Step)

#### Body
The body is worked in rounds, starting at the center.
1. **Foundation Round:** Ch 4, join to form a ring. (1)
2. **Rnd 1:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), work 11 dc into the ring, join with sl st to top of ch 2. (12 dc)
3. **Rnd 2:** Ch 2, 2 dc in each dc around, join with sl st. (24 dc)
4. **Rnd 3:** Ch 2, *dc in next dc, 2 dc in next dc*, repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (36 dc)
5. **Rnd 4:** Ch 2, *dc in next 2 dc, 2 dc in next dc*, repeat from * to * around, join with sl st. (48 dc)
6. **Rnds 5–15:** Ch 2, dc in each dc around, join with sl st. (48 dc for each rnd)
   - Repeat for Rounds 5–15 (11 rounds total).
7. **Rnd 16:** Ch 1, sc in each dc around, join with sl st. (48 sc)
8. **Rnd 17:** Ch 1, sc in next 12 sc, ch 1, skip 12 sc, sc in next 12 sc, ch 1, skip 12 sc, join with sl st. (48 sc)
9. **Rnds 18–19:** Ch 1, sc in each sc around, join with sl st. (48 sc)

#### Top
10. **Rnd 20:** Ch 1, sc in each sc around, have the last sc join with sl st to start.
11. Cut the yarn, leaving a long tail for sewing.

### Finishing
12. With tapestry needle, weave in all ends. 
13. Sew the button onto the center of the top, creating a decorative yet functional feature.
14. Optionally fill the body lightly with fiberfill if a sturdier shape is desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Pad Tower
- Use stitch markers to keep track of your rounds; it helps avoid mistakes.
- Take breaks to count your stitches regularly to ensure the right shape.
- Practice your tension by keeping your stitches consistent for a neater finish.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too tight." – This can happen if you're crocheting too tightly. Try relaxing your grip on the yarn.
- "The shape looks off." – If the size doesn't match, check your gauge and adjust your hook size if needed.
- "I have gaps between my stitches." – Ensure you’re joining rounds carefully and pulling the slip stitch tightly.

## How to Care for Your Pad Tower
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing to maintain its shape, ensuring your cotton pad dispenser lasts a long time.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes, you can swap out the yarn for any medium-weight cotton yarn, but note that different fibers may change the look and drape.

### How can I resize the dispenser?
To resize, adjust the number of rounds you crochet for the body. New rounds will follow the same increase pattern.

### How long will this take to make?
Expect to spend around 3 hours, divided between making the body, top, and finishing touches.
