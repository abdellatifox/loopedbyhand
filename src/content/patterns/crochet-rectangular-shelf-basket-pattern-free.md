## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- inc – increase (2 sc in same stitch)

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Dimensions:** 
  - Width: 12" (30 cm)
  - Depth: 10" (25 cm)
  - Height: 8" (20 cm)
- **Gauge:** 
  - 12 sc and 14 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in single crochet.
  
To check gauge, work a swatch of 20 stitches by 20 rows in single crochet. Adjust your hook size as needed to achieve the correct gauge.

## How to Crochet a Rectangular Shelf Basket (Step by Step)

### Bottom of the Basket
The bottom is worked first, creating the base for your basket.
1. **Ch 31.** 
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (30 sc)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
4. **Rows 3–6:** Repeat Row 2 (30 sc each row).

### Sides of the Basket (Changing Colors)
We’ll build up the sides of the basket now, changing to the contrast color.
5. **Row 7:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
6. **Row 8:** Change to CC. Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
7. **Rows 9–16:** Repeat Row 8 (30 sc each row).

### Creating the Basket's Height
Next, we'll add the height for your basket.
8. **Row 17:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
9. **Rows 18–25:** Repeat Row 17 (30 sc each row).

### Finishing the Basket
Finally, we’ll finish the edges to give a nice clean look.
10. **Row 26:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
11. **Row 27:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
12. **Row 28:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
13. **Row 29:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (30 sc)
14. **Row 30:** Sl st to the beginning of the row to create a clean edge. Fasten off and weave in ends.

### Assembly & Finishing
- Fold the basket into shape, ensuring the bottom is flat and all sides are even.
- Weave in all loose ends with a tapestry needle.
- Block the basket if desired, shaping it to the final dimensions.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Rectangular Shelf Basket
- Use stitch markers to mark the beginning of your rounds or rows—this will help keep track as you crochet.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to avoid mistakes and keep the basket even.
- If you find your stitches tight, relax your tension as you crochet for a more comfortable experience.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This may occur if you're gripping the yarn too hard. Relax your tension and try again!
- The shape looks off. – Make sure you’re counting stitches accurately. An even stitch count helps maintain the basket's shape.
- I’ve lost track of my rows. – Use a row counter or jot down your progress after each row to keep track.

## How to Care for Your Rectangular Shelf Basket
Hand wash your basket in cold water and lay it flat to dry. This will help maintain its shape and quality.
