This crochet Shelf Basket with a label window pocket is a stylish and functional way to organize your items at home. The Tag Basket allows you to label its contents easily, making it perfect for keeping things tidy. With simple stitches like single crochet and slip stitch, this project is enjoyable to make, and you’ll see progress quickly.

This basket is perfect for beginners looking to create practical storage solutions. As you work on this project, you'll gain skills such as crocheting in rows and attaching a vinyl pocket for labeling.

- Quick to complete – you'll have a stylish organizer in just around 4 hours.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern introduces easy stitches and basic techniques, making it a great choice for new crocheters.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st – stitch
- rpt – repeat
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same st)
- dec – decrease (single crochet two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished Size: Approximately 30.5 cm wide × 20.3 cm deep × 25.4 cm tall (12 in × 8 in × 10 in)
- Gauge: 14 sc and 12 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

## How to Crochet a Shelf Basket (Step by Step)

### Base
The base is worked first and consists of rows of single crochet. 
1. Ch 30.
2. Row 1: Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (29 sc)
3. Row 2: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (29 sc)
4. Row 3–12: Repeat Row 2 for 10 more rows. (29 sc)

### Sides
Next, we build the sides of the basket.
5. Row 13: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (29 sc)
6. Row 14: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each sc across. (29 sc)
7. Row 15: Ch 1, turn. Inc in the first st, sc in each st across until the last st, inc in the last st. (31 sc) 
8. Row 16: Repeat Row 15. (33 sc)
9. Row 17: Repeat Row 15. (35 sc)
10. Row 18–22: Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (35 sc)

### Label Window Pocket
Now we'll attach the vinyl for the label window.
11. Attach clear vinyl at the center of Row 10: 
    - Cut a 10 cm × 15.2 cm (4 in × 6 in) rectangle from the vinyl.
    - Place the vinyl over the center of the basket and pin in place.
    - Use a sl st to secure the vinyl to the crochet (work along three sides).

### Finishing the Sides
Let's complete the basket's sides.
12. Continue sc around the upper edge of the basket for 2 more rounds, increasing the st count evenly as needed to maintain the basket shape. (Approx. 38–40 sc)
13. Fasten off and weave in ends.

### Assembly & Finishing
- Cut a piece of cardboard measuring 5 cm × 10 cm (2 in × 4 in) for the tag.
- Insert the tag under the vinyl pocket for easy labeling.
- Weave in all remaining ends.
- If desired, lightly stuff the bottom of the basket with fiberfill to give it sturdiness.
- Block the basket lightly using steam to shape it if necessary.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Basket
- Keep your tension consistent for even stitches.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to avoid mistakes.
- Choose contrasting colors for a fun, decorative look and feel.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. This can happen if you are holding the yarn too tightly. Try relaxing your grip a bit to create a looser tension.
- The shape looks off. If the sides don’t seem to rise straight, you might need to reassess your increases. Check that you increase evenly on both sides.
- I'm confused about attaching the vinyl. If you find it tricky, try pinning the vinyl before sewing to keep it in place while you work.

## How to Care for Your Basket
To keep your basket in good shape, hand wash it in cold water and lay it flat to dry. Avoid wringing it out, as this can damage the basket and the vinyl.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes, you can use any worsted weight yarn. Just check your gauge to ensure the size remains consistent. 

### How do I resize the basket?
To make a larger basket, increase your starting chain by 10 stitches for every additional 2 inches in width you desire.

### How can I make the label pocket more secure?
For extra stability, you can sew the vinyl on all four sides instead of just three.
