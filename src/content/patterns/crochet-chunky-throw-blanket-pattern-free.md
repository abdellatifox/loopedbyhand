## Skill Level: Easy
Yes! This pattern is suitable for beginners who are familiar with basic stitches like single crochet and double crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- dc – double crochet
- sc – single crochet
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- RS – right side
- WS – wrong side

**Basket Weave Stitch:**
- Row 1: (RS) Ch 1, sc in first st, *dc in next 3 sts, sc in next 3 sts; repeat from * to last st, sc in last st.
- Row 2: (WS) Ch 1, sc in first st, *dc in next 3 sts, sc in next 3 sts; repeat from * to last st, sc in last st. Repeat these two rows alternately.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: Approximately 50 inches x 60 inches (127 cm x 152 cm).
- Gauge: 7 stitches and 8 rows = 4 inches (10 cm). 

To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 stitches by 20 rows in the basket weave stitch. If your gauge does not match, adjust your hook size or yarn selection.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
The blanket is worked in rows using the basket weave stitch. 

**Foundation:**
1. Ch 56 (this is the starting chain, to get a width of approximately 50 inches).

**Rows:**
2. Row 1: Work basket weave stitch using Color A. (54 sts)
3. Row 2: Work basket weave stitch using Color A. (54 sts)
4. Repeat Rows 1-2 for a total of 20 rows in Color A (40 rows overall).
5. Switch to Color B:  
   - Row 21: Work basket weave stitch using Color B. (54 sts)
6. Row 22: Work basket weave stitch using Color B. (54 sts)
7. Repeat Rows 21-22 for a total of 20 rows in Color B (40 rows overall).
8. Switch back to Color A:  
   - Rows 61-80: Continue alternating basket weave stitch in Color A for 20 rows (54 sts) again.

9. Fasten off after completing Row 80.

The blanket should now measure approximately 50 inches x 60 inches.

## Assembly & Finishing
- Weave in all ends using the tapestry needle.
- Block the blanket gently on blocking mats to achieve even edges and desired dimensions.
- Ensure edges are straight before final shaping.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Chunky Throw Blanket
- Choose a soft, medium to dark colored yarn to highlight the basket weave stitch.
- Count your stitches regularly to keep your blanket even and correct any mistakes quickly.
- If you're feeling unsure, practice the basket weave stitch on a smaller swatch first.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. This can happen if you're using too small a hook for the yarn. Try using a larger hook or relaxing your tension.
- The shape looks off. Make sure you are counting your rows and following the stitch pattern closely. Keep your tension consistent.
- I can't see the basket weave stitch. Ensure you're alternating the rows correctly and keeping to the defined stitch counts.

## How to Care for Your Chunky Throw Blanket
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry to maintain the quality of the acrylic yarn. This care will help keep your blanket looking its best.

## FAQ

### How do I resize the blanket?
To create a larger or smaller blanket, adjust the foundation chain in multiples of 6 for width, while keeping the same row count for length.
