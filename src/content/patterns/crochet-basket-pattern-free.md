## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- rnd – round
- inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)

## How to Increase (inc)
1. Insert your hook into the stitch.
2. Yarn over and pull up a loop.
3. Yarn over again and pull through both loops on your hook. That's one single crochet.
4. Repeat this in the same stitch for a second single crochet.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: Approximately 25 cm (10 in) in diameter and 20 cm (8 in) tall.
- Gauge: 12 stitches and 8 rows in single crochet = 10 cm (4 in).
- To check gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 stitches by 10 rows in single crochet. If your swatch does not match this gauge, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Body of Basket
The body is worked in rounds to create a sturdy basket.

1. **Foundation Round**: 
   - Ch 4, join with sl st to form a ring. 
   - 8 sc in the ring. (8 sts)

2. **Rnd 1**: 
   - Ch 1, inc in each st around. (16 sts)

3. **Rnd 2**: 
   - Ch 1, *(sc in next st, inc in next st)* repeat around. (24 sts)

4. **Rnd 3**: 
   - Ch 1, *(sc in next 2 sts, inc in next st)* repeat around. (32 sts)

5. **Rnd 4**:  
   - Ch 1, *(sc in next 3 sts, inc in next st)* repeat around. (40 sts)

6. **Rnd 5**: 
   - Ch 1, *(sc in next 4 sts, inc in next st)* repeat around. (48 sts)

7. **Rnd 6–15**: 
   - Ch 1, sc in each st around for 10 rounds. (48 sts each round)
   - Your basket should now be about 20 cm (8 in) tall.

8. **Rnd 16**: 
   - Ch 1, sc in each st around for a final round. (48 sts)

9. **Finish**: 
   - Fasten off. Weave in all ends.

### Stiffening Process
The stiffening will ensure your basket stands tall.

10. **Prepare Stiffening Solution**: 
    - Follow the instructions on your fabric stiffener to dilute it as needed in a bowl.

11. **Soak Basket**: 
    - Submerge the finished basket into the stiffening solution fully. Wring gently without stretching the shape.

12. **Shape and Dry**: 
    - Carefully mold the basket into the desired shape and place it on a foam surface to dry. Allow it to dry completely according to the stiffener’s instructions, typically around 24 hours.

### Assembly & Finishing
- Check that the basket has retained the desired shape after drying.
- Optionally, line the basket with cotton fabric for additional stability and aesthetic.
- Weave in any remaining ends and trim.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Basket
- Keep track of your stitch count to maintain the basket's shape.
- Use a stitch marker to mark your rounds if needed.
- Don't rush the drying process. A well-stiffened basket will serve you better.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This may lead to a smaller basket that doesn't stand well. Relax your tension, or try a larger hook.
- The shape looks off. – Ensure you’re following the round increase pattern closely. Review your stitch counts carefully.
- The stiffener is a mess. – Be careful not to over-soak the basket when stiffening. Less is often more.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! While this pattern uses worsted weight cotton, you can try other fibers, but keep in mind the stiffness might vary.

### How do I resize the basket?
To make a larger or smaller basket, adjust the number of rounds or the stitch count in your increases.

### Can I wash my basket?
Yes, but hand washing is recommended. Avoid soaking the stiffened areas. Spot cleaning is best to maintain its shape.
