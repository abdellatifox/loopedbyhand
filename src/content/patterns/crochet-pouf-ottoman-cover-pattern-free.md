## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- hdc – half double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- Rnd – round

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: approximately 46 cm (18 in) in diameter and 30 cm (12 in) in height
- Gauge: 8 hdc and 6 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To check gauge, make a small swatch of 20 hdc in a row for 15 rows, then measure. If it doesn't match, try a different hook or adjust your tension.

## How to Crochet a Pouf Ottoman Cover (Step by Step)

### Part 1: Base
The base is worked in rounds, starting with a magic ring. 
1. Start with a magic ring and ch 2 (this counts as your first hdc).
2. Round 1: 10 hdc in magic ring, pull tight, join with sl st to top of ch 2. (10 sts)
3. Round 2: Ch 2, (hdc in next st, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (15 sts)
4. Round 3: Ch 2, (hdc in next 2 sts, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (20 sts)
5. Round 4: Ch 2, (hdc in next 3 sts, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (25 sts)
6. Round 5: Ch 2, (hdc in next 4 sts, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (30 sts)
7. Round 6: Ch 2, (hdc in next 5 sts, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (36 sts)
8. Round 7: Ch 2, (hdc in next 6 sts, 2 hdc in next st) repeat around, join with sl st. (42 sts)

### Part 2: Sides
Next, we'll create the sides of the pouf.
9. Rounds 8–20: Ch 2, hdc in each st around, join with sl st. Repeat for a total of 12 rounds. (42 sts)

*You should have a height of approximately 30 cm (12 in) from the base at this point.*

### Part 3: Top
Finally, we'll finish the pouf cover.
20. Round 21: Ch 2, hdc in each st around, join with sl st. (42 sts)
21. Round 22: Ch 1, sc in each st around, join with sl st. (42 sts)
22. Fasten off and weave in all ends.

## Assembly & Finishing
If you're using stuffing or foam, fill the pouf to your desired firmness. Close the bottom with a slip stitch seam if needed. Remember to weave in any remaining ends and block lightly if desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Pouf Ottoman Cover
- Use stitch markers to help keep track of your rounds.
- Double-check your stitch counts at the end of each round to catch errors early.
- Don't be afraid to adjust your yarn tension for a more even look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. This can alter the size of your pouf. Try to work with a looser tension.
- The shape looks off. Make sure your stitch counts are correct for each round to keep the shape uniform.
- I can’t tell where to join. Use stitch markers to indicate the beginning of each round for better clarity.

## How to Care for Your Pouf Ottoman Cover
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry or spot clean as necessary. This will help maintain the integrity of the yarn and the shape of your pouf.
