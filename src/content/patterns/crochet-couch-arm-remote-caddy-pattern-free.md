This crochet Couch Arm Remote Caddy pattern makes a cozy and convenient accessory to keep your remotes, pens, and other small items within easy reach. With three pockets for organization, this caddy is not only practical but also a joy to crochet. We’ll be using basic stitches and repeating some simple rows, making it an enjoyable project for beginners.

This pattern suits anyone looking to declutter their living space while gaining confidence in their crochet skills. You’ll learn to work in rows, create pockets, and finish with a neat edging.

## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern uses basic stitches like single crochet and double crochet, making it accessible for those new to crochet.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- sp – space
- inc – increase (2 stitches in the same stitch)
- dec – decrease (single crochet two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge 
- Finished dimensions:
  - Couch arm caddy: approximately 30 cm (12 in) wide by 20 cm (8 in) tall
  - Pockets: each approximately 10 cm (4 in) wide by 15 cm (6 in) tall
- Gauge: 12 dc and 8 rows = 10 cm (4 in) in double crochet

## How to Crochet a Couch Arm Remote Caddy (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Base
The base is worked in rows, starting from a foundation chain.
1. **Foundation Chain:** Ch 40. (40 sts)
2. **Row 1:** Starting from the second ch from the hook, sc in each ch across. Turn. (39 sts)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 2 (counts as first dc), dc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
5. **Row 4:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
6. **Row 5:** Repeat Row 3. (39 sts)
7. **Row 6:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
8. **Row 7:** Repeat Row 3. (39 sts)
9. **Row 8:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
10. **Row 9:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
11. **Row 10:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (39 sts)
12. **Row 11:** Repeat Row 3. (39 sts)

### Step 2: Pockets (make 3)
These pockets are attached to the body of the caddy.
13. **Row 1:** Ch 20. (20 sts)
14. **Row 2:** Ch 2, dc in each ch across. Turn. (20 sts)
15. **Row 3:** Ch 1, sc in each st across. Turn. (20 sts)
16. **Row 4:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (20 sts)
17. **Row 5:** Repeat Row 3. (20 sts)
18. **Row 6:** Ch 2, dc in each st across. Turn. (20 sts)
19. **Row 7:** Repeat Row 3. (20 sts)
20. **Row 8:** Ch 1 and fasten off, leaving a long tail for sewing.

### Step 3: Assembly
We will sew the pockets onto the main body.
21. **Position Pockets:** Place the three pockets evenly on the body piece with the base of each pocket starting from Row 4 of the base. Pin in place.
22. **Sew Pockets:** Using the tapestry needle and the tail left from each pocket, sew the sides and bottom of each pocket onto the main body. Ensure the top edge is open.

### Step 4: Edging
The edging adds a nice finishing touch.
23. **Edging:** Using the contrast color (Light Gray), attach yarn at one end of the caddy. Work a sc evenly around the edge, placing 3 sc in each corner for smooth rounding.
24. **Join:** Sl st to the first sc to join. Fasten off and weave in all ends.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Couch Arm Remote Caddy
- Count your stitches after each row to keep your work even.
- Use stitch markers to mark the start of each row if needed.
- Don’t rush! Take your time to enjoy the process and check your work as you go.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This can happen if you’re holding the yarn too tightly. Try to relax your grip as you work.
- The shape looks off. – This usually means you might have missed a stitch. Double-check your work, especially at the start of each row.
- The pockets are collapsing. – This may occur if they aren’t sewn securely. Make sure to stitch the pockets firmly to prevent sagging.

## How to Care for Your Couch Arm Remote Caddy
Hand wash in cool water and lay flat to dry. Avoid placing it in a dryer to maintain its shape and structure.

To adjust the size, you can modify the foundation chain length to fit your couch arm’s width. Just ensure to keep the foundation chain a multiple of your gauge.
