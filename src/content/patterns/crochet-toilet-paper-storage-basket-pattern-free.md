## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- Hdc – half double crochet
- dc – double crochet
- sk – skip
- st – stitch

## How to Make a Magic Circle
1. Hold the yarn to form a loop, with the working yarn in the back.
2. Insert your hook through the loop from front to back.
3. Yarn over and pull up a loop.
4. Yarn over again and pull through the first loop on your hook.
5. Continue with your pattern.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Base
The base is worked in rounds, starting with a magic circle.  
1. Make a Magic Circle. (1 circle)  
2. Ch 1, 8 sc in the circle. Pull the circle tight. (8 sc)  
3. Round 2: 2 sc in each st around. (16 sc)  
4. Round 3: *1 sc in next st, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. (24 sc)  
5. Round 4: *1 sc in next 2 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. (32 sc)  
6. Round 5: *1 sc in next 3 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. (40 sc)  
7. Round 6: *1 sc in next 4 sts, 2 sc in next st; repeat from * around. (48 sc)  

### Sides
Next, we’ll create the sides of the basket.  
8. Round 7: Ch 1, sc in each st around. (48 sc)  
9. Round 8: Ch 1, switch to Color B, *2 Hdc in next st, 1 Hdc in next 5 sts; repeat from * around. (56 Hdc)  
10. Round 9: Ch 1, switch to Color A, *2 dc in next st, 1 dc in next 6 sts; repeat from * around. (64 dc)  
11. Round 10: Ch 1, sc in each st around. (64 sc)  
12. Round 11: Ch 1, switch to Color B, *sc in the next st, skip 1 st; repeat from * around. (32 sc)  
13. Round 12: Ch 1, sc in each st around. (32 sc)  
14. Round 13: Ch 1, switch back to Color A, sc in each st around. (32 sc)  

### Finishing
15. Fasten off and weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.  
16. If desired, place a cardboard disc in the bottom of the basket for stability.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Toilet Paper Storage Basket
- Use a stitch marker to keep track of your rounds.
- Count your stitches at the end of each round to ensure proper shape.
- For a neater appearance, block your basket after finishing.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This can make the basket hard to work with. Try using a larger hook or relaxing your tension.
- The shape looks off. – Ensure you’re following the stitch counts and checking your gauge; adjusting your hook size can help.
- I can’t keep my yarn ends from showing. – Make sure to weave in your ends securely. You can also crochet over them in the final rounds.

## How to Care for Your Toilet Paper Storage Basket
Handwash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid wringing or twisting the basket to maintain its shape.

### How do I make the basket larger or smaller?
Adjust the number of initial rounds or change the stitch counts evenly to scale up or down.
