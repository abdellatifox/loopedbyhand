This crochet Laptop Sleeve that Opens Into a Desk Mat (The Desk Fold) is a practical and stylish project that combines function with creativity. The sleeve provides protection for your laptop and transforms into a spacious desk mat when unfolded. You'll enjoy making this project as you practice half double crochet (hdc) and become familiar with creating buttonholes.

This pattern is suitable for adventurous beginners looking to tackle a slightly more challenging project. As you work through it, you'll enhance your skills and gain confidence in your crochet abilities.

- Chic and modern – the simple style adds elegance to your workspace.
- Create custom colors – personalize it to match your taste.

## Skill Level: Intermediate
This pattern is ideal if you have some crochet experience. You should be comfortable with basic stitches like half double crochet and be able to create buttonholes. 

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- hdc – half double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- sc – single crochet
- dec – decrease (work 2 sts together)

## How to Make a Buttonhole
1. For button placement, mark where the front flap aligns with the body sleeve on each side.
2. On the marked side, ch 1, sc in first 3 sts, ch 2, skip 2 sts, sc in the last 2 sts.

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions when folded: 15" x 10" (38 cm x 25 cm)
- Finished dimensions when unfolded: 30" x 20" (76 cm x 51 cm)
- Gauge: 14 stitches and 10 rows = 4 inches (10 cm) in hdc.

To check your gauge, crochet a swatch of 20 hdc across and 14 rows high. Adjust your hook size if necessary to match.

## Step-by-Step Instructions
### Body of the Sleeve
The body is worked in rows to create a sleeve that fits your laptop.

1. Foundation Row: Ch 50, hdc in 3rd ch from hook and in each ch across (48 hdc).
2. Row 1: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in each st across (48 hdc).
3. Row 2: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in each st across (48 hdc).
4. Rows 3–20: Repeat Row 2 for 18 more rows. You should have a total of 20 rows (48 hdc each).
5. Row 21: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in next 15 sts, ch 5 (this will be the flap), hdc in last 33 sts (48 hdc total, 15 hdc plus 5 chain).
6. Row 22: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in each st across first 15 hdc, 5 hdc in the chain space, hdc in remaining 33 hdc (48 hdc total).
7. Row 23: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in each st across (48 hdc).
8. Row 24: Ch 2, turn. Hdc in each st across (48 hdc).
9. Row 25: Ch 1. Finish with a round of sc around the entire piece for a clean edge; sl st to join.

### Final Touch and Buttonholes
Now we’ll add buttonholes and finalize the design.

10. Buttonholes: Mark where the front flap aligns with the body sleeve on each side. Follow the buttonhole instructions above.
11. Row 26: Ch 1, sc around the edge and in between the button holes to finish.

### Unfoldable Desk Mat
To create the foldable mat function, unfold the sleeve. The hdc fabric will create a non-slip surface that can now be used as a desk mat.

### Assembly & Finishing
- Sew the buttons on the opposite side of the buttonholes using a tapestry needle and the main color yarn.
- If using fleece, pin the fleece backing to the crochet mat (optional). Trim fleece to match the dimensions 30" x 20".
- Weave in any yarn ends securely with a tapestry needle.
- Block the finished piece to shape and define.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Laptop Sleeve
- Double-check your gauge before starting to ensure the sleeve fits your laptop properly.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to help avoid mistakes.
- Consider using a stitch marker to keep track of your rows.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. Ensure you’re not pulling the yarn too tightly as you crochet. Consider using a larger hook.
- The shape looks off. Regularly check your rows to ensure you're maintaining your stitch count.
- I can’t seem to get the buttonholes right. Practice the buttonhole technique on scrap yarn before adding it to your project.

## How to Care for Your Laptop Sleeve
Hand wash in cool water and lay flat to dry. Cotton yarn is durable but avoid the dryer to prevent shrinking.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! You can choose any worsted weight cotton yarn in colors you love. Just keep in mind that gauge may change.

### How do I resize this pattern?
To resize, adjust your foundation row chain length. Keep your stitch multiples consistent and check your gauge.

### How long will this project take?
The entire project typically takes about 6–7 hours, including finishing touches.
