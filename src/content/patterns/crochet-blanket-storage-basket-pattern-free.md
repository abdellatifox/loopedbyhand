## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- Ch – chain
- Sc – single crochet
- Hdc – half double crochet
- Sl st – slip stitch
- Inc – increase (2 sc in the same stitch)
- Dec – decrease (sc two stitches together)

## Size and Gauge
- Finished size: 
  - Approximately 30 cm (12 in) tall
  - 35 cm (14 in) diameter
- Gauge: 
  - 9 sc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in)
  
Check your gauge by making a swatch of 10 rows and 10 stitches in single crochet. If it doesn’t match, you can adjust your hook size for proper measurements.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Basket Body
The body is created in rounds starting from a magic circle.
1. **Foundation Round:** With MC, make a magic circle, ch 1 (not considered a stitch), and work 8 sc into the circle. Pull tight and join with a sl st to the first sc. (8 sts)
2. **Round 1:** Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with a sl st. (16 sts)
3. **Round 2:** Ch 1, *sc in next st, inc in next* repeat around. Join with a sl st. (24 sts)
4. **Round 3:** Ch 1, *sc in next 2 sts, inc in next* repeat around. Join with a sl st. (32 sts)
5. **Round 4:** Ch 1, *sc in next 3 sts, inc in next* repeat around. Join with a sl st. (40 sts)
6. **Round 5:** Ch 1, *sc in next 4 sts, inc in next* repeat around. Join with a sl st. (48 sts)
7. **Rounds 6–12 (Add height):** Ch 1, sc in each st around, join with a sl st. (48 sts)  
   Repeat for 7 rounds.

### Step 2: Handles
Handles are made separately and attached to the basket.
8. **Create Handles:** Switch to 5 mm hook.
9. **Row 1:** Ch 1, sc in next 20 sts, ch 12 for the handle, skip next 8 sts, sc into next 20 sts. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
10. **Row 2:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
11. **Row 3:** Ch 1, sc in next 20 sts, ch 12, skip 12, sc in remaining 8. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
12. **Row 4:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
13. **Row 5:** Ch 1, sc in next 20 sts, ch 12, skip 8, sc in next 12. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
14. **Row 6:** Ch 1, sc in each st around. Join with sl st. (40 sts)
15. **Fasten off** and weave in ends.

### Assembly & Finishing
- If lining, cut fabric to match the basket's interior dimensions. Pin and sew the lining to the top, folding the top edge inward for a clean finish.
- Weave in all yarn ends securely.
- Block the basket lightly to reshape it if needed.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Blanket Storage Basket
- Take your time to count your stitches in each round to avoid mistakes.
- Use stitch markers to keep track of the beginning of rounds.
- Don’t rush; enjoy the process and allow yourself to feel comfortable with each stitch.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My basket is not holding its shape. This could be due to loose tension; try to keep your stitches uniform and not too loose.
- The base is too small. If your foundation round is not wide enough, it may be due to missing increases; check your counts.
- My handles are uneven. Ensure you follow the same number of stitches on both sides, and use a ruler to check their length.

## How to Care for Your Blanket Storage Basket
To maintain the shape of your basket, hand wash it in cold water. Reshape it and let it air dry flat. Avoid machine washing as it can distort the basket's form.
