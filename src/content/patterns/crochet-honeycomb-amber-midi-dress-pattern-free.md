## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- sp – space  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- sc – single crochet (insert hook, yarn over, pull up loop, yarn over, draw through both loops)  
- hdc – half double crochet  
- dc – double crochet (yarn over, insert hook, pull up loop, yarn over, draw through 2 loops, yarn over, draw through remaining 2 loops)  
- tr – treble crochet  
- RS / WS – right side / wrong side  
- yo – yarn over  
- rep – repeat  
- BLO – back loop only  
- FPdc – front post double crochet (work around the vertical post of the indicated stitch instead of its top loops; insert hook from front to back to front, complete dc normally)  
- BPdc – back post double crochet (work around the vertical post from back to front to back, complete dc normally)  
- sc2tog – single crochet two together (insert in next stitch, yo and pull up loop; repeat in following stitch; yo and draw through all three loops)  
- Shell – 5 dc into one stitch  
- Picot – ch 3, sl st into first chain; decorative, not a base stitch

## How to Crochet a Honeycomb Amber Midi Dress (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Main Stitch Pattern for Body Panels
With a 4.0 mm hook and the main color, ch 102 for each panel (99 stitches). 
- **Row 1 (RS)**: Dc in the fourth ch from hook and each ch. [99 sts]  
- **Row 2 (WS)**: [Dc in next 2 sts, BPdc around next post] 33 times. [99 sts]  
- **Row 3 (RS)**: [Dc in next st, FPdc around next 2 posts] 33 times. [99 sts]  
- **Following rows**: Repeat Rows 2 and 3. [99 sts]  

This stitch pattern will create the lovely honeycomb texture.

### Step 2: Front Dress Panel
Work the main stitch pattern through Row 132. [99 sts]  
- **Row 133**: Work next 33 positions for the first shoulder, leaving the remaining unworked. Turn. [33]  
- **Rows 134 to 140**: Continue over these 33 positions, preserving pattern columns. Fasten off. [33 each row]  

Leave the center 33 positions unworked. Join the main yarn at the outer edge for Row 133 and work 8 rows to match. [33 each row]  

### Step 3: Back Dress Panel
Work a second 99-position panel through Row 138. [99 sts]  
- **Rows 139 to 140**: Work outer groups of 33 positions separately for 2 rows, leaving center 33 unworked. Fasten off. [33 per shoulder each row]  
The back neck will be shallower than the front to ensure a proper fit.

### Step 4: Sleeves (Make Two)
With main yarn and a 4.0 mm hook, ch 78. Work the sleeve repeat through Row 48; fasten off.  
- **Row 1 (RS)**: Dc in fourth ch from hook and each ch. [75 sts]  
- **Row 2 (WS)**: [Dc in next 2 sts, BPdc around next post] 25 times. [75 sts]  
- **Row 3 (RS)**: [Dc in next st, FPdc around next 2 posts] 25 times. [75 sts]  
- **Following rows**: Repeat Rows 2 and 3. [75 sts]  

### Step 5: Belt
With main yarn and a 3.5 mm hook, ch 10.  
- **Row 1**: Sc in second ch and each ch. [9 sc]  
- **Rows 2 to 320**: Ch 1, turn, sc BLO in every st. [9 sc each row]  

Target a length of 160 cm and a width of approximately 4.5 cm. 

### Step 6: Assembly
Using the main-color yarn and a tapestry needle, mattress stitch to join front and back panels at the shoulders, matching row ends.  
- **Join 1**: [33 paired stitches per shoulder; 2 seams]  
- **Join 2**: Mark row ends for sleeves, sew sleeves in place.  
- **Join 3**: Close underarms and sides.  

### Step 7: Edgings and Details
For neck, cuffs, and hem, switch to a 3.5 mm hook. Begin with sc along neckline and cuffs. 
- **Neck Round 1**: Attach at back neck, sc into different positions. Join. [106 sc]  
- **Neck Round 2**: Ch 3, dc in each sc, join. [106 dc]  
- **Cuffs**: Follow similar steps for cuff rounds.  
- **Hem**: Sc into positions, adding shells as noted for a scalloped edge.  

### Finishing
Damp-block flat and check all seams. 

## Beginner Tips for a Great Honeycomb Amber Midi Dress
- Block your swatch multiple times to ensure correct gauge.
- Use markers to help keep track of your rows and stitches.
- Try on your dress before adding the final edging to ensure a perfect fit.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- "My stitches are too loose." – This might happen due to using a larger hook. Try switching to a smaller hook and rework your swatch.
- "The hem pulls too tight." – If your hem isn't lying flat, check for dropped stitches or consider increasing the hook size slightly.
- "The neckline is too small." – Ensure your gauge is correct before beginning. If it feels tight, you may need to adjust your row count.

## How to Care for Your Honeycomb Amber Midi Dress
Hand wash cool using mild detergent that's compatible with your yarn. Support the dress while it's wet, then roll it in a towel and lay it flat to dry. Avoid using heat on acrylic materials, and keep any embellishments secure before washing.
