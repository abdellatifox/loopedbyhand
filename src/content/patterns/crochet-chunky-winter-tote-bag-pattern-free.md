## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain  
- dc – double crochet  
- sl st – slip stitch  
- st(s) – stitch(es)  
- sc – single crochet  
- sk – skip  

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Dimensions:**
  - Approximately 38 cm (15 in) wide, 30 cm (12 in) tall, 15 cm (6 in) deep
- **Gauge:** 
  - 8 dc and 5 rows = 10 cm (4 in)

To check gauge, make a 10 cm × 10 cm (4 in × 4 in) swatch in double crochet. If your gauge doesn't match, adjust your hook size accordingly.

## How to Crochet a Chunky Winter Tote Bag (Step by Step)

### Body of the Bag
The body is worked in rows, starting with the foundation chain.
1. Foundation Row: Ch 45 (44 sts and 1 for turning).
2. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from the hook and in each ch across (43 dc).
3. Row 2: Ch 3, turn, dc in the next dc and in each dc across (43 dc).
4. Rows 3–13: Repeat Row 2 for a total of 13 rows.
5. Row 14: Ch 1, turn, sc in first dc and in each dc across (43 sc).

### Sides of the Bag
Next, we work the sides to give it structure.
6. Row 15 (Start Sides): Ch 1, turn, sc in first sc and in each sc across (43 sc).
7. Row 16: Ch 1, turn, sk first sc, sc in next sc, then *ch 1, sk next sc, sc in next sc* repeat to end (22 sc + 21 ch-1 spaces).
8. Row 17: Ch 1, turn, sc in first sc, then *sc in ch-1 space, sc in next sc* repeat to end (43 sc).
9. Rows 18–23: Repeat Row 16 and Row 17 for a total of 6 rows.

### Finishing the Tote
To finish the body of the bag.
10. Row 24: Ch 3 (counts as dc), turn, dc in each space across for 43 dc.
11. Row 25: Ch 1, turn, sc in first dc, *ch 1, sk next dc, sc in next dc* repeat to end.
12. Row 26: Ch 1, turn, sc in first sc and in each sc across (43 sc).

### Straps
Now, let’s create the shoulder straps.
13. Strap Row 1: Ch 10 (9 and 1 for turning).
14. Row 1: Dc in 4th ch from hook and in each ch across (7 dc).
15. Rows 2–10: Repeat Row 1 for a total of 10 rows.
16. Fasten off leaving a long tail for attaching the straps to the bag. Make 2 straps.

### Assembly
- Fold the bag body in half with the right sides facing out.
- Use a slip stitch or sc to seam the sides together, leaving the top open.
- Use the tapestry needle to weave in any ends.
- Attach the straps to the bag by sewing them securely at the top corners of the bag (approx. 7.5 cm (3 in) from the side seams).

## Beginner Tips for a Great Chunky Winter Tote Bag
- Ensure your tension is consistent to avoid uneven stitches.
- Check your stitch count regularly to maintain the shape as you work.
- If you find your bag is too large or small, adjust the foundation chain in multiples of 2.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. This may be due to tension; try relaxing your grip or using a larger hook.
- The shape looks off. Incorrect stitch counts can distort your project, so count your stitches in every row.
- I'm not sure where to attach the straps. Measure about 7.5 cm (3 in) from the side seams for an even look.

## How to Care for Your Chunky Winter Tote Bag
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid exposing your bag to direct heat sources like sunlight to maintain its shape and texture.

## FAQ

### What if I want a smaller bag?
To create a smaller bag, simply reduce the foundation chain in multiples of 2 to keep the shape proportionate.
