## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- hdc – half double crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)

## Size and Gauge
- **Gauge**: 14 hdc and 10 rows = 10 cm (4 in).
- **Finished Sizes**:
  - Size 2: Chest 61 cm (24 in), Length 36 cm (14 in)
  - Size 4: Chest 66 cm (26 in), Length 38 cm (15 in)

  - Size 8: Chest 76 cm (30 in), Length 46 cm (18 in)
  - Size 10: Chest 81 cm (32 in), Length 51 cm (20 in)

## How to Crochet a Kids Cardigan (Step by Step)

### Body of Cardigan
The body is worked in rows, starting with the foundation chain.
1. **Foundation Chain**: Make a chain of 66 (70, 74, 78, 82) for sizes 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 respectively.
2. **Row 1**: Hdc in the 3rd ch from the hook, hdc across (64, 68, 72, 76, 80 sts).
3. **Row 2-16**: Ch 2, hdc in each st across (64, 68, 72, 76, 80 sts). At the end of row 16, switch to CC.
4. **Row 17-20**: Ch 2, hdc in each st across (64, 68, 72, 76, 80 sts).
5. **Row 21**: Ch 2, hdc in the first 32 (34, 36, 38, 40) sts, chain 14 (for the armholes), skip 28 (30, 32, 34, 36) sts, hdc in the last 32 (34, 36, 38, 40) sts.
6. **Row 22**: Ch 2, hdc in each hdc and the chain across (64, 68, 72, 76, 80 sts).
7. **Row 23-31**: Repeat rows 2-10 for body length. Switch back to MC at the end of row 31.
8. **Row 32**: Ch 2, hdc in each st across (64, 68, 72, 76, 80 sts). Fasten off.

### Sleeves
The sleeves are worked separately and sewn onto the body.
1. **Chain for Sleeves**: Make a chain of 36 (38, 40, 42, 44).
2. **Row 1**: Hdc in the 3rd ch from the hook and hdc across (34, 36, 38, 40, 42 sts).
3. **Row 2-10**: Ch 2, hdc across (34, 36, 38, 40, 42 sts). Fasten off.
4. **Attach Sleeves**: Align sleeves to armholes, sew or whip stitch in place.

### Pockets
The pockets add a practical touch to the cardigan.
1. **Chain for Pockets**: Make a chain of 21.
2. **Row 1**: Hdc in 3rd ch from hook and across (19 sts).
3. **Row 2-8**: Ch 2, hdc across (19 sts). Fasten off.
4. **Attach Pockets**: Position on the body of the cardigan; sew 2 pockets on each side approximately 15 cm (6 in) from the bottom.

### Assembly & Finishing
- **Joining**: Join sleeves with the body using a whip stitch or slip stitch.
- **Edging**: Optionally, add a row of sl st around the cardigan edges for a neat finish. 
- **Weaving in Ends**: Weave in all ends with a tapestry needle.
- **Blocking**: Lightly steam block for better shape if desired.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Kids Cardigan
- Use stitch markers to track your rows and keep your work organized.
- Count your stitches at the end of each row to maintain the correct shape.
- Don't hesitate to reverse a few rows if you feel uncertain about your work.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. – This often happens if you're nervous. Try to relax your grip or use a larger hook. 
- The shape looks off. – Check your stitch count; it’s easy to miss a stitch. 
- It feels too small. – Gauge is key! Always make a swatch before starting to ensure your measurements match.

## How to Care for Your Kids Cardigan
Machine wash cold and tumble dry low. Avoid bleach to keep your cardigan looking fresh.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes! You can use any worsted weight yarn, but be sure to check the gauge so it fits properly.

### How do I adjust the size?
To adjust the size, add or remove stitches in your foundation chain, and use the stitch counts for the desired size as a guide.

### How long will it take to finish?
Expect to spend about 4–5 hours total, with the body taking the most time.
