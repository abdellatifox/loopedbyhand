## Is This Pattern Good for Beginners?
Yes! This pattern is beginner-friendly. You'll learn the Tunisian simple stitch and how to change colors while achieving a lovely finished product.

## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- st – stitch
- tss – Tunisian simple stitch
- Return Row – the row worked to return your stitches to the front of the work

## Size and Gauge
- Finished dimensions: 18 in × 18 in (45 cm × 45 cm)
- Gauge: 10 stitches and 12 rows = 4 in (10 cm) in Tunisian simple stitch
- To ensure the right fit, check your gauge by crocheting a small swatch.

## How to Crochet a Pillow Cover (Step by Step)
The body is worked in rows, starting with the foundation.

### Part 1: Foundation and Body
1. **Foundation Row**: Ch 22 with Color A. Pull up a loop in the 2nd ch from the hook and each ch across (21 loops on the hook).
2. **Return Row**: Yarn over and pull through 1 loop (1 ch). *Yarn over, pull through 2 loops* repeat until 1 loop remains on the hook. (21 sts)
3. Change to Color B.
4. **Row 1**: With Color B, tss across, ending with 21 loops.
5. **Return Row**: Yarn over and pull through 1 loop, then *yarn over, pull through 2 loops* across. (21 sts)
6. Repeat Rows 4–5, alternating between Color A and Color B until the piece measures 18 in (45 cm) in height.
7. Finish with a Return Row using Color A.

### Part 2: Edging
8. **Edging Row**: With Color A, ch 1, and work 1 sc into each edge row around (including corners: 3 sc in each corner). Join with a slip stitch to the first sc.

## Finishing Instructions
- Weave in all ends using a tapestry needle.
- Place the pillow form inside the cover.
- Optional: Add buttons or ties for closure.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Pillow Cover
- Use the recommended yarn weight for the best results.
- Count your stitches regularly to keep your work even.
- Keep a consistent tension for a professional look.

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are uneven. – This can happen if your tension varies. Try to keep it consistent, especially when switching colors.
- The cover is too small. – Double-check your gauge. If it doesn't match, adjust your hook size.
- I'm having trouble with color changes. – Practice switching colors in a swatch first until you feel comfortable.

## How to Care for Your Pillow Cover
To keep your pillow cover looking nice, hand wash in cold water and lay it flat to dry.

## FAQ
### Can I use a different yarn?
Yes, you can experiment with different yarns. Just be aware that it may affect the gauge and finished size.

### How do I change colors?
To change colors, finish the last stitch of the previous row with the new color in place of the old one during the return row.

### Can I make this larger or smaller?
Absolutely! Adjust the foundation chain to change the width and modify the number of rows for the height.
