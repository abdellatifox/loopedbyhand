## Crochet Abbreviations (US Terms)
- ch – chain
- sc – single crochet
- sl st – slip stitch
- st(s) – stitch(es)
- HDC – half double crochet

## How to Half Double Crochet (HDC)
1. Yarn over and insert your hook into the stitch.
2. Yarn over again and pull up a loop (three loops on your hook).
3. Yarn over one last time and pull through all three loops on your hook.

## Size and Gauge
- **Finished Dimensions:**
  - Small: 9 in (23 cm) length, 5 in (12.5 cm) width at cuff
  - Medium: 10 in (25 cm) length, 6 in (15 cm) width at cuff
  - Large: 11 in (28 cm) length, 7 in (17.5 cm) width at cuff
- **Gauge:** 10 sc and 6 rows = 4 in (10 cm). If your gauge swatch is larger, switch to a smaller hook; if smaller, switch to a larger hook.

## How to Crochet Chunky Slipper Socks (Step by Step)

### Main Body
The body of the sock starts with a foundation chain and is worked in rows. 
1. **Foundation:** Ch 22 for small, 24 for medium, or 26 for large.
2. **Row 1:** Sc in 2nd ch from hook and in each ch across. (21 sts for small, 23 sts for medium, 25 sts for large)
3. **Row 2:** Ch 1, turn. HDC in each st across. (21 sts for small, 23 sts for medium, 25 sts for large)
4. **Row 3:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in first 10 sts, then HDC in the next 11 (or 13 for large). (21 sts for small, 23 sts for medium, 25 sts for large)
5. **Row 4:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in first 11 sts, HDC in next 10 sts. (21 sts for small, 23 sts for medium, 25 sts for large)
6. **Repeat Rows 3–4** until the piece measures 6 in (15 cm) from foundation chain for small, 7 in (18 cm) for medium, and 8 in (20 cm) for large.

### Heel
The heel section is designed to add comfort and support.
7. **Row 1:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in first 10 sts, leave remaining sts unworked. (10 sts for small, 11 for medium, 12 for large)
8. **Rows 2–4:** Ch 1, turn. Sc in each st across. (10 sts for small, 11 for medium, 12 for large)
9. Fasten off, leave a long tail for sewing.

### Instep
The instep is attached after completing the main body and heel.
10. For the instep, with right side facing, rejoin yarn at the first unworked stitch of the body.
11. Ch 1. Sc in the first st, then HDC in remaining stitches across to the end. (21 for small, 23 for medium, 25 for large)

### Cuff
The cuff is worked in the back loops only, giving it a delicate look.
12. **Row 1:** Ch 1, turn. Working in back loops only, sc in each st across for 5 rows. (21 for small, 23 for medium, 25 for large)
13. Fasten off and leave a long tail for sewing.

## Assembly & Finishing
- Fold the sock in half, aligning the heel with the instep. 
- Use a whip stitch to sew the sides together from the heel to the cuff, ensuring it's snug but not too tight.
- Weave in all ends with a yarn needle.
- If desired, block the socks by lightly steaming to stretch them to the final shape.

## Beginner Tips for a Great Chunky Slipper Socks
- Check your gauge before starting to ensure the socks fit well.
- Keep your tension even for a neat finish; this helps with consistency in the stitches.
- Don’t hesitate to customize colors; use your favorite yarn to make these socks your own!

## Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)
- My stitches are too tight. This often happens if you're holding the yarn too tightly. Try relaxing your tension and counting your stitches.
- The shape looks off. If your socks are not the right length, you might need to add or remove rows in the body section.
- I'm not sure how to seam properly. Check videos on whip stitching; practice can quickly improve your technique.

## How to Care for Your Chunky Slipper Socks
Hand wash in cold water and lay flat to dry. Avoid soaking to maintain firmness and ensure your socks last longer.
